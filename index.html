Used Car Buying Guide & FAQ (All 50 U.S. States)

Free Buyer Guide

Whether you are buying a used Honda, Toyota, Ford, or truck from Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, OfferUp, or an independent lot, follow this pre-purchase checklist before handing over cash.

How do I spot a used car scam on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist?

Paste the listing description and seller messages into Step 1 above. Watch out for "bill of sale only" or missing titles, sellers claiming to be "selling for a friend" (curbstoning), urgent "cash today / leaving town" pressure, Zelle or gift-card deposits before seeing the car, and "easy fix" check-engine light excuses.

What does an official U.S. NHTSA VIN check show?

Step 2 connects directly to official U.S. Department of Transportation (NHTSA) APIs to decode the 17-digit VIN, confirm model year, trim, body style, engine, and assembly plant, and list active safety recalls plus owner complaint counts for that model year.

Why follow the 15 → 5 → 3 → 1 used-car buying funnel?

Checking every car in person wastes weekends and inspection fees. Screening 10–15 listings free with AI eliminates obvious traps, letting you run VIN checks only on your Top 5 clean contenders, compare Top 3 finalists, and pay a mechanic for a pre-purchase inspection (PPI) on just your 1 Core Pick.

Does a clean listing or NHTSA recall check replace a mechanic inspection (PPI)?

No. Always match the dashboard VIN to the driver door-jamb sticker and the paper title in the seller's legal name, take a highway and city test drive, and have an independent mechanic perform a lift and OBD-II scan before signing.
