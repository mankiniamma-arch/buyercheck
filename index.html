Frequently Asked Questions

Why screen 15 listings free before running VIN checks?

Traditional vehicle reports cost $40–$45 each. Filtering out missing titles, curbstoners, and shady ads free with AI first ensures you only run VIN checks on your Top 5 clean contenders and pay a mechanic for a Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI) on your #1 pick.

Where does the VIN recall and complaint data come from?

Step 2 connects directly to official U.S. Department of Transportation (NHTSA) public APIs to decode the 17-digit VIN, factory specifications, open safety recalls, and owner complaint counts.

Does BuyerCheck replace an in-person mechanic inspection (PPI)?

No. Always verify the paper title matches the seller's photo ID and dashboard VIN, take a test drive, and have an independent mechanic perform a Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI) before paying cash.
